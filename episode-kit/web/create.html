YOUR WORDS. YOUR DISCOVERY.

Start with your own cards.

Give your puzzle a scene, two to eight card names, and an action. Then write its ordering clues in a fresh workbench.

Your existing workbench and its drafts stay where they are. This page creates a separate original episode.

01 · SET THE SCENE

A reason to arrange the cards.

This controls the generated clue wording. Changing the interface does not translate your writing.

02 · CHOOSE THE WORDS

Names that carry an action.

Use two to eight names, each starting with a letter. Keep every card on its own line, without empty lines. This list does not declare a solution order.

Choose what the ordered initials should spell. The workbench will check your clues and action together.

Make room for the reasoning.

The starting episode has no clues or hints. It is intentionally unfinished: add clues and check the discovery in the workbench.
