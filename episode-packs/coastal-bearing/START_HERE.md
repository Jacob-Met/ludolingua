# Play Coastal Bearing

Explore a short coastal navigation puzzle in English or its authored Spanish adaptation. Read the scene, examine the cards, and work out the direction they indicate.

## Start the application

You need Node.js 22 or later and a browser. From the extracted bundle:

    cd episode-kit
    node serve.mjs

Open the local address printed by the server.

## Choose an episode

In the author view, select **Reopen an export** and open either file:

- English: `episode-packs/coastal-bearing/exports/episodes/01-en-source.json`
- Spanish adaptation: `episode-packs/coastal-bearing/exports/episodes/03-es-adapted.json`

These paths start at the extracted bundle folder.

Select **Open a separate playtest** to play with the author controls concealed. For the least spoiled experience, have someone else load the episode and open the playtest for you.

## Keep your play record

After playing, select **Download play record** (or **Descargar registro de juego** in Spanish). Keep the JSON file to revisit the session. In the author view, **Reopen a play record** verifies and displays it.

## Optional review — spoilers

Save `episode-packs/coastal-bearing/REVIEW.md` until after playing.

The file `02-es-literal-contrast.json` is a deliberate failed localization contrast for reviewers, not the recommended Spanish play experience.

To inspect a comparison, select **Reopen a comparison** in the author view and choose a file from `episode-packs/coastal-bearing/exports/comparisons/`.

This original case was authored with ChatGPT. The Spanish adaptation has not been assessed by a native Spanish-speaking human reviewer.
